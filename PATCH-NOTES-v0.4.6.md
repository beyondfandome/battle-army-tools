# Battle Army Tools v0.4.6

- Adds GM-only **Import Crown Army**.
- Imports Crown troop composition and siege equipment; Crown Onagers map to Battle Catapults.
- Creates/reuses a Battle commander/General and carries Crown player ownership, House, army ID/name into deployed units.
- Import is snapshot-only and does not alter Crown strategic manpower.
- Adds GM-only **Battle Strength** for live survivor counts at any time.
- Battle Strength groups Crown imports by strategic army/House and ordinary battles by commander/formation/team.
- Soldier formations count current manpower; individual pieces count as pieces.
