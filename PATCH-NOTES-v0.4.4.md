# Battle Army Tools v0.4.4

## Ballista
- Added Ballista as an individual Siege Engine.
- Stats: 50 HP, 8 Attack, 2 Defence, Range 10, Movement 0, Manpower 1.
- Ballista has 5 ammo and its attacks count as ranged siege projectiles.
- Full Attack against normal units; half Attack (rounded up) against Walls and Palisades.
- Siege Crew grants +3 Defence against ranged attacks.
- Impaling Bolt: spend 1 Command Point once per round to double Attack against War Beasts (Dragon, Elephants, Mammoth).
- Ballista appears in deployment and Scene Unit Balance and is treated as an individual piece.
- Reset Battle restores Ballista ammunition and ability state.
