# Crown Overview Tools v0.5.8

## Embark / disembark movement rule

- An army **may move first and then embark** later in the same turn.
- Embarking consumes all movement the army and commander have remaining.
- Once embarked, neither the army nor commander can continue land movement that turn.
- Disembarking still consumes the entire movement allowance for that turn.
- After disembarking, the army and commander remain movement-locked until movement refreshes.
- Navy movement remains separate.
