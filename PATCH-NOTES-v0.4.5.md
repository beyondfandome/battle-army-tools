# Battle Army Tools v0.4.5 — Heavy Siege Reloading

- Catapult range increased from 14 to 18.
- Trebuchet range increased from 14 to 24.
- Catapults and Trebuchets are now Heavy Siege weapons: after firing, they spend the following battle round reloading and cannot attack during that round. They are ready again on the next round.
- Heavy Siege reload state is shown in the battle-unit tooltip, including the round in which the weapon becomes ready.
- Heavy Siege still spends exactly 1 ammo per shot; Incendiary Ammo and Barrage do not increase ammo cost or reload duration.
- Ballista remains Range 10 and can fire every round; Battering Ram remains melee and does not reload.
- Reset Battle clears Heavy Siege reload state and restores siege ammunition as before.
