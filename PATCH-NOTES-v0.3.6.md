# Battle Army Tools v0.3.6

- Added a GM **Next Turn** control to the Battle Turn Tracker.
- The GM can manually advance from the current person/side to the next entry in turn order.
- Using Next Turn on the final entry stops at **All turns complete** and still requires **Advance Round**; it does not automatically start a new round.
- GM manual advancement is announced in chat and recorded as a GM override in the turn-state metadata.
