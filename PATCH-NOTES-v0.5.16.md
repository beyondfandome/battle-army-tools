# Battle Army Tools v0.5.16

## Vision rule change

- Every deployed battle unit has Foundry vision enabled.
- Every battle unit uses a fixed vision range of **12**.
- Generals no longer use a special vision distance.
- Siege Engines no longer gain vision equal to weapon Range.
- Long-range weapons can still retain their normal attack Range, but targets beyond 12 must be revealed by another friendly unit/scout.
- Existing battle units on a scene are normalized to vision 12 when the GM loads the scene.
- Newly deployed troops and newly created Generals are created with vision 12 immediately.

All v0.5.14-v0.5.15 timer and assignment changes are retained.
